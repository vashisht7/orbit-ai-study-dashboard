What the examples establish

The 29 small Python labs teach individual mechanisms. The optional transformer uses a repeated synthetic corpus and does not establish independent generalization or production quality. Browser controls calculate the stated toy equations; they are not live NoteEchoes inference.

NoteEchoes evidence

Project-specific facts reuse the source-backed review from 15 September 2026: model and adapter configurations, pinned Swift runtime identity, prompt and schema code, action-provider validation, retrieval services and recorded evaluation reports. They are a snapshot, not a claim that all installed builds or later revisions match.

Open the detailed source index in the NoteEchoes companion. Current language-support comments and the pinned release documentation have inconsistent labels; this course does not infer verified multilingual accuracy from a comment.

How to use the references

The book explains the concepts locally. References are for verifying implementation-specific details, exploring further and checking evolving protocols. You do not need to open all of them to complete the beginner lessons.
